**F5 — Secret-leak, `smoke-test.yaml`, and no-SDK guards contain concrete bypasses across the Story 31.1 ↔ 31.2 boundary. CONFIRMED.**

- **Unanchored `"uid"` substring bypass (`OpenBaoPlatformDocumentationTests.cs:1264-1265,1281`):** `FindUnexpectedUuids` checks `PublishedIdentifierMarkers.Any(marker => line.Contains(marker, StringComparison.OrdinalIgnoreCase))`. Because `"uid"` is a substring of `"uuid"`, `"guide"`, `"guidance"`, and `"guid"`, any leaked UUID-shaped credential (e.g., AppRole `role_id`/`secret_id` or Dapr token) on a line containing any of those words bypasses AC3 detection completely.
- **URL-safe base64 & paragraph-wide digest exemptions (`OpenBaoPlatformDocumentationTests.cs:1241,1246,1298-1307`):** `FindUnexpectedLongRuns` excludes `-` and `_` (splitting URL-safe base64 tokens below 40 chars), requires all 3 character classes (missing single-case non-hex secrets), and `HasPublishedDigestContext` excuses any long run inside an entire blank-line-delimited prose paragraph containing common words (`"chart"`, `"manifest"`, `"commit"`, `"revision"`, `"head"`).
- **Unscanned Epic 31 evidence files (`OpenBaoPlatformDocumentationTests.cs:1210-1220`, `RuntimeSecretStoreMigrationTests.cs:394-400`):** The 12 Nov 16–17 Story 31.1 evidence `.md`/`.json` files, `deploy/openbao/server-tls-identities.cnf`, all 9 `spec-31-*.md` files, and `31-2-runtime-dapr-secret-store-migration.md` are excluded from the AC3/AC2 secret-safety scans.
- **`smoke-test.yaml` single-key projection regex bypass (`OpenBaoPlatformDocumentationTests.cs:386-390`):** Asserts `Regex.Matches(smokeTest, @"^\s+- key: ").Count == 1` and `ShouldNotContain("- key: tls.key")`. Listing `path:` before `key:` (`- path: tls.key\n  key: tls.key`) or double-spacing (`-  key: tls.key`) mounts the server private key into the throwaway status pod with the suite green.
- **No-SDK scan exempts two packable libraries (`RuntimeSecretStoreMigrationTests.cs:330`):** `BoundaryProjects = ["AppHost", "Aspire", "Redis"]` skips `Hexalith.Memories.Aspire` and `Hexalith.Memories.Redis` from both `RejectProviderPackages` and `RejectProviderReference`, even though `Hexalith.Memories.Aspire` is explicitly documented as provider-neutral (`openbao.md:485-489`). Additionally, `RejectProviderPackages` (`:376`) misses compound names without a delimiter before `vault` (e.g., `Azure.Security.KeyVault.Secrets`), and nested `src/Directory.Build.props` is unscanned (`:363`).
