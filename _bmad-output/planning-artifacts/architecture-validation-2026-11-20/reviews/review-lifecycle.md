| Severity | Finding and consequence | Proposed disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | **Erasure verification for an empty target.** AD-16 lines 207–209 require a sampled record known to exist before deletion for every physical target. A tenant that never exported anything has no source-held export record to sample. The rule gives the verifier no way to complete that tenant's erasure truthfully. | Amend AD-16 to distinguish a populated target from a provably empty target. For the latter, require a recorded, generation-fenced search over the complete tenant scope. | High | **Erasure retry after key destruction.** AD-16 lines 209 and 215 require completed key destruction followed by a recheck of `Deleting` and its generation before appending the tombstone. The decision leaves the surviving authoritative proof of the generation unspecified. | Specify a durable, content-free proof of the committed deletion revision and generation and bind each target acknowledgement to it. Restrict its authority to resuming that deletion. Demonstrate a restart after the key becomes unusable and before the tombstone append. Preserve the single idempotent completion event. |
| High | **Conflicting lifetimes for retained telemetry.** AD-6 line 141 and the ledger at line 472 require erasing the tenant telemetry resource with the tenant. AD-16 lines 209 and 212 complete tenant erasure after a handoff and preserve opaque records through their TTL. AD-17 line 221 allows Platform Operations to count and purge those records after erasure. An implementation that drops the retained partition at tenant deletion defeats that contract. | State which tenant credentials and principal-token mapping are revoked or purged during erasure, which records and locator survive under Platform Operations, and who retires the empty partition. Test deletion while opaque records still have time remaining. Align Story 34.32 with the resulting order. |
| Medium | **Destination of the retention purge receipt.** AD-17 line 221 and its ledger row at line 494 place the receipt in the erased-tenant register. AD-21 lines 268–272 assign the register's dedicated stream and monotonic sequence to erasure lineage and give retention accounting separate authority over purge records. Two writers can select different streams and disagree on the register revision. | Direct the scoped retention writer to a distinct record stream on the platform partition. Verify that a purge receipt is readable by its authorized reader and leaves the erased-tenant register revision unchanged. |
